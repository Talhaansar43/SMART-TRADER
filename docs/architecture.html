2 · Request flow: one click on the Analysis page

Typing a ticker triggers two parallel calls. The analysis call fans out to market data providers, then hands everything to the strategy service for number crunching.

3 · The paper trading bot

A simulator, not a broker. It runs a trend-breakout strategy on a $1,000 paper account, saves every trade with its reason to a SQLite file, and resumes by itself after a backend restart.

A · Start-up when you press Start, and again after every restart
1
Set the account in the UI

Starting cash (default $1,000), max positions, tick interval, stocks and/or crypto.

2
POST /api/trades/bot/start

Pydantic validates the request, a new run is saved in SQLite, then a background asyncio task starts.

3
Load the universe

SPY and BTC first (market filters), then 17 more crypto pairs and the S&P 500 list from a GitHub CSV.

4
Resume after a restart

On app start, restore() reloads the last run's cash, positions and cursor and continues if it was running.

B · Every tick 10 s by default, about 66 ticks (11+ min) per full pass
5
Take the next 8 symbols

A cursor walks the list so provider rate limits are respected.

6
Fetch 420 days of bars

Yahoo Finance, 8 threads in parallel. Finnhub is skipped because candles return 403.

7
Compute indicators

trend_strategy.py: 55-day high, 20-day low, 200-day average, ATR and 6-month momentum.

8
Exits now, entries at pass end

Held symbols are checked for exits as scanned. Breakout candidates are ranked and bought when the pass completes.

9
Save and log

Each trade and the new account state commit in one transaction, with the reason in plain language.

10
UI polls every 3 s

GET /bot/status feeds the account, positions, trade history, reasons and log.

BUY

Close above the 55-day high and the 200-day average, positive 6-month momentum, and the market filter (SPY or BTC above its 200-day average) is on. Strongest momentum first. Risk 1.5% of equity per trade, stop 2 ATR below entry, at most 20% of equity per position.

SELL

Close below the 20-day low (trend ended) or at or below the protective stop. The whole position is sold and the realized P&L is saved with the reason.

HOLD

Anything else. Winners are not capped, so a few big trends pay for many small stops (about one in three trades wins in backtests).
